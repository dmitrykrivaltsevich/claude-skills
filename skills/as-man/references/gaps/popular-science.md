# Gaps — popular science, history, trade non-fiction

Applies to: popular science, history of technology and science, narrative non-fiction for a general reader.

## Expected coverage

| Probe | What a reader needs |
|---|---|
| Simplification cost | Which simplification is not merely incomplete but actively misleading. |
| The primary source | What the cited researcher actually claimed, compared with the book's rendering. |
| Contested history | Attribution and priority disputes presented as settled fact. |
| Survivorship | Whether the chosen story is representative or was chosen because it worked out. |
| Narrative pressure | Where the shape of a good story bends the account: a lone genius, a clean turning point, a decisive experiment. |
| Current state | Whether the science described still stands. |
| Certainty gradient | Which parts are established, which are the author's reading, which are speculation. |
| Retrospective coherence | Whether the past is described as leading inevitably to the present. |
| Absent contributors | Work omitted because it does not fit the narrative or was historically overlooked. |

## Genre traps

- **The anecdote is doing the argument's work.** A vivid story stands in for evidence, and the reader remembers the story.
- **Hero framing.** Discovery is collective and slow; books need protagonists.
- **The quotation with no source.** Famous lines in this genre are frequently apocryphal.
- **Analogy that outlives its usefulness.** A metaphor introduced to explain becomes the reader's model of the mechanism.
- **Chapter conclusions that overreach the chapter.**

## Example

```
GAP 1 - The account presents a disputed attribution as settled

Kind:         contradicted
Materiality:  medium
Missing:      The book credits a single figure with the result. Priority is
              disputed, and at least two independent lines of work reached it
              in the same period.
Effect:       A reader repeats an attribution that specialists will identify
              as wrong, and misses that the idea was arrived at independently.
Evidence:     [unverified - model knowledge, confirm before acting]
```
